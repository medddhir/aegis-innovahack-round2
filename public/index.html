WHY AEGIS IS DIFFERENT

It does not trust the agent to obey.

Controls live outside the agent, so a buggy, compromised or overzealous system cannot simply ignore them.

01

Task-bound money

Funds are limited to an explicit purpose, approved vendors and expiry.

02

Adaptive defence

Permissions tighten automatically as behaviour becomes suspicious.

03

Evasion awareness

Related transactions are evaluated as a coordinated sequence, not isolated requests.

04

In-flight control

Pending intents are revalidated and can be revoked before settlement.

05

Policy simulation

Teams can prove a control works before enforcing it.

06

Forensic proof

Every approval, block and freeze is evidence-backed and replayable.
