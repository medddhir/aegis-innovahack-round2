The scores below use the 21 reviewed captures in `docs/screenshots/black-label/`, the seven-viewport browser audit, the 32-component audit, and the verified functional suites as evidence. A score is not awarded for a component merely existing; it must clarify the product and remain usable in mobile and reduced-motion modes.

| --- | ---: | --- |

| Sophistication | 9.5 | Command/Research modes, the layered Core, policy scanner, evidence terminal, docks, and network diagram share one precise material and state language. |
