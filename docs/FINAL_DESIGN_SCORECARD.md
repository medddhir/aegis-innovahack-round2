## Gate review

- No reviewed screen resembles a component showcase; legacy feature, architecture, duplicate contract, and closing-marketing sections are removed from the visible narrative.
- The contact sheet reads as one visual system: obsidian canvas, warm-white editorial type, cyan structure, and semantic colour only at actual outcomes.
- No primary viewport is horizontally crowded, and no required screenshot depends on animation to communicate its outcome.
- All financial outcomes, Twin comparisons, ledger evidence, risk states, policy versions, and funds-moved values remain engine- or proof-derived.
- The only WebGL scene is progressive; the SVG/CSS Instrument remains the complete fallback.
- No score is below 9. The weakest category—mobile density—was refined by flattening depth, preserving a sticky action, and keeping technical evidence below the primary transaction state.
